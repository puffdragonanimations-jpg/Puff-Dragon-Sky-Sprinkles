Privacy Policy – Puff Dragon: Sky Sprinkles

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Puff Dragon: Sky Sprinkles (“the game”) is made by Red Arrow Ltd.

Data we collect: none. The game does not ask for an account, does not collect personal information, and does not send your gameplay data to us or to anyone else. Your progress (donuts, upgrades, best scores and settings) is saved only on your device and is removed when you uninstall the game.

Advertising: the game contains no ads.

Third-party services: the game is distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play. Apple and Google may process data under their own privacy policies (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/ and https://policies.google.com/privacy) when you download or update the game.

Children: the game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes: if this changes in a future version, for example if ads or purchases are added, we will update this page before that version is released.
